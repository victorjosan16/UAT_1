2. CORE BOARD
Portrait orientation. One continuous CLOSED ROUNDED-RECTANGLE conveyor LOOP surrounds a central play area. Colored small cubes travel around this same perimeter in one direction, including all four rounded corners. There are NO vehicles, NO wheels, NO cars. Inner stationary trays contain colored compartments and are oriented vertically or horizontally around the loop. Additional tray queue at the bottom. The loop itself stays fixed in position; cubes travel along it.

3. COLOR-CUBE CONVEYOR MECHANIC
Represent every cube as a logical item with stable unique ID, color ID, path-distance coordinate, state and reservation. Drive all cubes around a closed path using arc-length parametrization, constant world-space speed and deltaTime; smooth corner interpolation; stable spacing without overtaking or overlap. Cubes wrap around continuously until matched. Rendering can use pooled meshes/sprites but gameplay must be deterministic.

4. TRAYS / MATCHING
Each tray has a sequence of colored compartments (example: magenta, peach, green, magenta). A tray is stationary at its assigned station while active. When a passing cube matches the next available required compartment at that station, reserve it and animate a clear transfer from conveyor to tray. Remove that cube from the circulating set ONLY on successful arrival. A mismatched cube stays on the conveyor and continues circulating. Trays complete when every required compartment is filled; play a completion animation, update level objective, clear the station, and allow a replacement tray. If the reference video clearly shows an alternative transfer direction or ordering, document that observation and adjust the rule consistently, with tests.

5. PLAYER INTERACTION
Bottom queue contains trays awaiting placement. Tap an eligible tray to send it smoothly to a compatible empty station, with clear preview/selection feedback. Do not allow selecting covered or locked trays if reference rules restrict them. Reserve station and tray atomically to prevent double-tap duplication. If no station is free, do not consume the tray. Completed trays leave their station and free it. Empty trays or trays with no required cargo must follow an explicit and tested lifecycle; never silently disappear. Include a clear loss/deadlock policy with an optional retry, not a softlock.

6. SPECIAL SLOTS / BLOCKERS
Implement the numbered grey tiles and green plus slots from the screenshot as configurable level elements. Numbered blockers unlock according to LevelData rules (do not invent universal meaning); green plus slots unlock or add station capacity according to explicit configuration. Show their requirements in UI. Make each mechanic optional per level and testable.

Ensure every required tray compartment has a matching cube and every level is solvable under queue and station constraints. Include a validator and reproducible simulation tests.
